Conscience — Ethical Reasoning

It has never been easier to justify a choice you already want to make. Whatever you’re inclined to do, AI will hand you a polished and persuasive argument for doing it. So the fact that you can defend a decision proves nothing. The real test is whether the decision still looks right when you examine it from the angles you would rather skip.

How It Works
01
Name It
State the situation and the instinct you are already leaning toward.
02
Examine It
Examine your instinct through four lenses. They test what that decision would do for you, who it lands on, what rule it implies, and who it makes you.
03
Test It
Make the strongest case that your instinct is wrong.
04
Decide
Give a verdict and make the call. Later, come back and record whether your instinct deserved to be trusted.
