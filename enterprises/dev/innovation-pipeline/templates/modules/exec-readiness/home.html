90 Days to Executive Readiness

Most organizations don’t fail at AI because of technology. They fail because their leaders lack the mindset, structure, and discipline to lead it. This program builds all three over ninety days, and leaves behind tools that keep working long after the sprint is done.

The 90-Day Plan
01
Assess
Diagnose leadership readiness, map the landscape, surface risks and mindset foundations.
02
Build
Design leadership structure, collaboration principles, and competency framework.
03
Develop
Run the fluency sprint, decision simulations, and middle management activation, and build your values architecture.
04
Embed
Install operating rhythm, rewire evaluation, complete the first leadership review.
05
Lead
Ongoing: govern the Leadership Dashboard with structure, L&D, and personal development.
