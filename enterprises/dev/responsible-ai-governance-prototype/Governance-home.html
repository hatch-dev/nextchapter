90 Days to Responsible AI Governance

Almost every organization is racing to deploy AI systems. Almost none are building the structures needed to govern them. This framework turns AI governance from an aspiration into a working discipline, spanning risk management, Responsible AI principles, and regulatory compliance. You build the machinery in 90 days. The discipline lasts as long as AI is part of your organization.

The 90-Day Plan
01
Map
Take stock of where you stand, inventory your AI systems, identify the risks, and map the rules that govern you.
02
Build
Write your Responsible AI principles, delegate governance activities, set the review cadence, and select risks for further assessment.
03
Assess
Screen risks for responsible AI concerns, assess selected risks in depth, classify the risk portfolio, and set up your testing protocols.
04
Embed
Develop risk management and monitoring plans, wire governance into daily operations, review every system, then hold the first comprehensive Governance Review.
05
Govern
Ongoing: maintain the risk portfolios, track compliance obligations, keep evidence logs current, and hold standing reviews.
