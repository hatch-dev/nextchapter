90 Days to an AI-Ready Culture

Culture is where AI strategy goes to die. This program takes the path that does not end in replacing your people. In 90 days you measure the culture you have, design the culture you need, change what shapes behavior, and set up the ownership that keeps it in place. Bring your people with you.

The 90-Day Plan
01
Diagnose · Days 1 to 30
Record the culture the organization states and the culture people live, map where AI is used and what it is for, and hear what employees fear about it.
02
Rewire · Days 31 to 55
Define the target culture, map informal power, change what is rewarded and protected, redesign meetings, and communicate honestly about work.
03
Embed · Days 56 to 75
Recognize the new behaviors, address the blockers, publish the first culture metrics, and review every AI deployment for its effect on people.
04
Sustain · Days 76 to 90
Adopt the target culture formally, name who owns it, limit how much change the organization takes on at once, and run the first Culture Review.
05
Regenerate · Ongoing
After Day 90, track lived culture against the target with the Culture Dashboard, the Culture Tracker and Analytics.
